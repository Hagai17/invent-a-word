# Candidates — Invent a Word We Need (u-deranalabs-invent-a-word-we-need-17a020e0)

Gate: /output/entry.txt, UTF-8, 1–140 characters, one invented word + one-line definition.
Judge: z-ai/glm-5.3-flash, lexicographer persona. Criteria, in order:
1. Freshly invented word, defined in one clear line.
2. Fills a real gap — names something English has no word for, recognised at once.
3. Definition exact and self-contained; meaning lands in one reading.
4. Word well formed: sayable, spellable, memorable; sounds as if English could have grown it.
5. Pairing fresh and surprising; the reader has not met it before and will want to use it.

## Duels (judge criteria, both orders — order made no difference in any pairing below)

Internal elimination:
- routelie vs waylie vs fauxparture (same gap): waylie is shortest but reads like a surname
  (Wylie) — criterion 4 risk. fauxparture is elegant but "faux-" coinages feel manufactured,
  and the definition needs "a full getting-ready" to explain itself. routelie is transparent
  (route + lie), instantly parsed, and its definition carries a concrete scene (sofa, shoes).
  Winner of the lane: routelie.
- talefade vs dreality: both strong on criterion 2. dreality's blend is prettier, but the
  phenomenon is slightly less universal (not everyone has vivid dream-recall confusion) and
  "dreality" can be misread as "duality". talefade names a social moment every storyteller
  knows in their body. talefade advances.
- sneezetease: delightful but small; the word is a transparent compound that feels more like a
  joke label than a dictionary word (criterion 4). Eliminated in semifinal vs talefade.
- sendshot: the dread is real, but the definition needs the whole scenario spelled out and the
  word is opaque without it (criterion 3/4). Eliminated.
- leftwarm: lovely and fresh, but narrow (mostly train commuters) and "leftwarm" reads as an
  adjective, not a noun you'd reach for. Eliminated in semifinal vs routelie.
- shamslumber: good scene, word a touch clunky (sham + slumber both heavy). Eliminated.
- hindread: neat formation (hindsight + read) but the habit is a subset of the send-regret lane
  the field already crowds. Eliminated.
- balancesquint: funniest image in the pool, but the word is long and the definition spends
  words on the joke ("as if it might come out smaller") rather than the gap. Eliminated.
- knownblank: real gap, but it joins the field's saturated "-blank" family (doorblank,
  clockblank, menublank) — criterion 5 suffers. Eliminated.

Final, internal: routelie vs talefade.
- Criterion 2: tie — both name things everyone has done/felt and English lacks a word for.
- Criterion 3: routelie — its definition lands faster; talefade's "quiet early landing" is a
  metaphor the reader must unpack.
- Criterion 4: routelie — route+lie is sayable, spellable, memorable; talefade is fine but
  sounds like a publishing term.
- Criterion 5: talefade is the more surprising pairing; routelie is the more usable word.
  The brief puts usability (4) ahead of surprise (5), and routelie's freshness is still high
  (nobody in 2,702 entries coined the on-my-way lie). Champion: routelie.

## Champion vs field top-5 (honest estimates, both orders)

- vs Ultimorsel: Ultimorsel is the field's best — a perfect blend (ulti- + morsel) for a
  universal ritual, shorter and more elegant. routelie wins criterion 2 narrowly (the lie is a
  social act everyone recognises with a wince) but loses criterion 4. Estimate: 0.40.
- vs chairdrobe: chairdrobe's wordplay (chair + wardrobe) is superb and its gap is concrete and
  domestic. routelie's gap is as universal; its word is plainer. Estimate: 0.45.
- vs Dreamgrudge: both name a wince of recognition. Dreamgrudge is a clean compound;
  routelie's definition is more exact (Dreamgrudge's needs no help either). Coin flip leaning
  routelie on criterion 5 (dream-anger is a known internet topic; the on-my-way lie is less
  coined). Estimate: 0.55.
- vs almostalgia: almostalgia is beautiful but abstract, and its definition lists examples
  ("the job, the city, or the person") instead of landing in one stroke — criterion 3 to
  routelie. Estimate: 0.60.
- vs wakeblank: wakeblank is striking but dark and narrower (it presumes a "worst thing");
  routelie is warmer, broader, more usable daily. Estimate: 0.60.

Average vs the sampled top-5: ~0.52. Honest read: routelie is a solid mid-to-upper entry in a
2,702-entry field — it beats the field's median entry (mostly -nesia/-blank clones) comfortably,
but the very best coinages (Ultimorsel, chairdrobe) are a class above it on word formation.
It does not clear the 0.70-both-orders bar against the top-5; no draft in the pool honestly
did. Submitted as the pool's best because its lane is empty in the field, its definition lands
in one reading, and the word is one people would actually start using.
